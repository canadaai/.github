# Canada AI

**An independent open research initiative advancing responsible, sovereign, and transparent AI in Canada.**

Canada AI focuses on Canadian AI policy analysis, bilingual and Canadian benchmark datasets, and independent ecosystem research.

### Status
This organization is currently preparing its initial public research releases. See our roadmap below for upcoming repositories and initiatives.

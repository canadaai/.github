## Hi there 👋

**Canada AI** is an open, community-driven initiative focused on strengthening artificial intelligence research, tooling, education, and responsible innovation across Canada.

We support developers, researchers, policymakers, and educators by building open-source resources that advance AI literacy, transparency, experimentation, and accessible technology for all Canadians.

## 🤖 What We Build

- **Open-Source AI Tools:** Lightweight libraries, utilities, and reference implementations for machine learning, data workflows, evaluation, and applied AI development.
- **Datasets & Research Resources:** Public datasets, documentation, benchmarking materials, and educational content that help accelerate Canadian AI research and learning.
- **Responsible AI Projects:** Guidance frameworks, transparency tools, ethics checklists, and open models that support safe, fair, and accountable AI use in Canada.
- **Developer & Learning Resources:** Tutorials, examples, technical guides, and reproducible workflows designed to make AI more accessible to new practitioners across the country.

## 🍁 Vision

Canada thrives when technology is open, ethical, and inclusive.

Canada AI aims to build a national ecosystem that supports open-source innovation, accessible AI education, responsible and transparent AI practices, community-driven research, and tools that empower individuals, developers, and organizations.  

We believe AI should serve the public good — not just private interests.

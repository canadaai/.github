# Canada AI Roadmap

Canada AI focuses on **independent Canadian AI research, policy evaluation, and bilingual/regional open datasets**.

## Active Repositories (Phase 1)
* *Initial repositories currently in preparation—no public transfers yet.*

## Planned Repositories (Phase 2)
* **`canadian-ai-policy-tracker`** — Structured open dataset and timeline of federal and provincial AI regulations, directives, and public consultations.
* **`bilingual-eval-bench`** — English-French Canadian domain evaluation benchmarks for institutional and public-interest AI systems.

## Future Exploration (Phase 3)
* Independent annual state-of-Canadian-AI technical reports and open sovereignty infrastructure assessments.
